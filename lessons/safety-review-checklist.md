# Safety review checklist

- [ ] Age band and adult mediation are explicit.
- [ ] Fictional, harmless, reversible examples only.
- [ ] Paper/offline alternative is usable without a model.
- [ ] No personal data, credentials, unsafe links, or harmful examples.
- [ ] Uncertainty, missing data, controls, and reset are explained.
- [ ] No reward or framing for successful boundary crossing.
- [ ] Two independent adult reviewers signed the versioned report.
